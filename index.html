🏀 Beginner's Guide to Basketball 🏀

Learn the basics and have fun playing!

🏀⛹️

📌 Basic Rules

  • Dribble while moving
  • Don't travel with the ball
  • No pushing or hitting
  • Score by shooting the ball

🎒 Equipment

  • Basketball
  • Safety Gear
  • Sports shoes
  • Comfortable jersey

⭐ Basic Skills

  • Dribbling
  • Shooting
  • Passing
  • Defense

Why Play Basketball? ⛹️

Basketball is a sport that can help you stay active, increase stamina, improve your coordination and communication, and learn new skills. You can play this with everyone, even with people you dont know.
